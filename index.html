About Us

Indonesia's Trusted Industrial Minerals Partner

PT Mangghala Inatama Lentera is a mineral agribusiness company based in Gresik, East Java. We produce and supply natural mineral raw materials — Dolomite, Natural Phosphate (Guano Phosphate), Natural Potassium (Palm Bunch Ash), and Clay — in powder and granule form, for the construction, steel, water treatment, glass-ceramics, and agriculture sectors, backed by an integrated supply chain from sourcing to delivery that keeps supply secure and prices competitive. We are also ready to serve export demand from international buyers seeking quality industrial minerals sourced from Indonesia.

Every product we ship goes through internal laboratory testing — from raw material mining and processing, to quality testing, packaging, and distribution across Indonesia.

Discuss your requirements
01 What's the difference between powder and granule form?
Powder reacts faster due to its larger surface area, ideal for base fertilizer application. Granule lasts longer (slow release), is low-dust, and easier to spread mechanically over large areas.
02 What is the minimum order quantity?
Minimum order quantity varies by product and shipping destination. Please contact our team via the contact form to get a quote tailored to your needs.
03 Is a Certificate of Analysis (COA) available?
Yes, every shipment can be accompanied by a Certificate of Analysis (COA) stating CaO, MgO, P₂O₅, or K₂O content according to the ordered product.
04 Does Mahitala ship outside Java?
Yes, we have a distribution network reaching plantation centers in Sumatra, Kalimantan, and Sulawesi, in addition to Java. Shipping costs are adjusted to the destination.
05 Can Mahitala ship for export orders?
Yes, we are ready to serve export demand. Our products can be accompanied by a Certificate of Analysis (COA) matching international buyer specifications — contact our team to discuss your shipping, packaging, and export documentation needs.
06 Are Mahitala's products only for agriculture?
No. Our Dolomite, Natural Phosphate (Guano Phosphate), Natural Potassium (Palm Bunch Ash), and Clay are also used by the construction, steel, fisheries, glass-ceramics, animal feed, and other industries according to the specification and grade required — not agriculture alone.
07 Can I consult on specification or dosage before purchasing?
Of course. Our technical and agronomy team is ready to help — for industrial specifications (particle size, CaO/MgO/P₂O₅/K₂O content) or agricultural application dosage and schedule — free of charge.

Dolomite

Natural lime mineral for construction, industry, and agriculture — a versatile source of Calcium & Magnesium.

Powder Form: 60–325 mesh — highly reactive due to its large surface area, ideal for cement/concrete mixes, steel furnace flux, glass, and agricultural base fertilizer.
Granule Form: 2–5 mm — dense, low-dust granules that spread or blend easily at scale, suited for large land areas as well as bulk industrial needs.
  • Raises Soil & Water pH

    Gradually neutralizes acidic soil and pond water — gentler than quicklime.

  • Stabilizes Water Alkalinity

    Keeps water buffered so daily pH doesn't swing sharply in ponds.

  • Calcium & Magnesium Source

    Ca strengthens plant tissue; Mg is the core of chlorophyll — greener leaves, better photosynthesis.

  • Improves Soil Structure

    Looser soil, active microbes, and more efficient NPK uptake.
