Our Story

History & Company Milestones

Founded in 2016 in Surabaya with a simple vision: to provide quality plant nutrition solutions that support sustainable agriculture and build long-term food security.

2016

Company founded; began producing Dolomite Soil Conditioner for the export market and supplying Natural Phosphate raw material to local manufacturers.

2017

Partnered with a small-scale fertilizer manufacturer to produce Natural Phosphate Fertilizer and market it directly to buyers.

2020

Built our first dedicated production facility for Natural Phosphate Fertilizer.

2021

Launched our first Dolomite Soil Conditioner brand for the local market.

2024

Expanded production of Natural Potassium Fertilizer made from palm bunch ash.

2026

Building our first dedicated production facility for Dolomite Soil Conditioner and Clay binder.

From Mine to Delivery

Our Production Process

1

Raw Material Sourcing

Raw materials come from our own sources: dolomite and natural phosphate mines, plus palm bunch ash from our own incinerator.

2

Grinding

Ground into 60–325 mesh powder to buyer specifications.

3

Granulation

Powder is formed into 2–5 mm granules, then dried to the hardness and moisture content specified.

4

Quality Control

A representative sample from every lot is lab-tested to confirm quality meets specification.

5

Packaging

Packed in 25 kg bags, 50 kg bags, or 1-ton jumbo bags as needed.

6

Delivery

Delivered by our dump truck fleet domestically, or consolidated for export shipment.
